1. Worked Numerical: Series RLC Resonance

Problem: $R=10\;\Omega$, $L=25\;\text{mH}$, $C=10\;\mu\text{F}$. Find $f_0$, $Q_0$, and $BW$.

Solution: $\omega_0 = 1/\sqrt{25\times 10^{-3}\times 10\times 10^{-6}} = 2000\;\text{rad/s} \Rightarrow f_0 = 318.31\;\text{Hz}$.
$Q_0 = \omega_0 L / R = (2000\times 0.025)/10 = 5$.   $BW = f_0 / Q_0 = 63.66\;\text{Hz}$.

2. Key Derivation: ABCD Reciprocity

From $V_1 = A V_2 - B I_2$ and $I_1 = C V_2 - D I_2$: $Z_{21} = 1/C$ and $Z_{12} = (AD-BC)/C$. Equating $Z_{12}=Z_{21}$ gives $AD - BC = 1$.
